# Project profile

- **Project name:** mexico-displacement
- **Topic:** Forced internal displacement in Mexico: its scale, causes, the most affected groups and places, and the legal and policy response.
- **Period covered:** 2018–2025
- **Audience:** Researchers and policy staff preparing a briefing on internal displacement in Mexico.
- **Decision it informs:** Where support and further research should focus.
- **Main questions:**
  1. How large is internal displacement in Mexico, and how is it changing?
  2. What drives it?
  3. Which groups and places are most affected?
  4. What is the legal and policy response?
- **Themes:**
  - scale-and-trends
  - causes
  - affected-groups-and-places
  - policy-and-response
- **Language of sources and outputs:** English
- **Created on:** 2026-10-16
