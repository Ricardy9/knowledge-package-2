# Start here

This kit helps you research a topic using a set of documents. It keeps every finding linked to the exact source and page it came from.

You put your documents in, an AI guide sets up your workspace by asking you a few questions, and NotebookLM (a free Google tool) answers your research questions using only your documents. The AI guide then files every answer and finding for you, so nothing gets lost and everything can be checked.

You do not need to know how to code.

## What you need
- A computer (Windows or Mac).
- A free Google account, to use NotebookLM at notebooklm.google.com. (Google may show it under the name "Gemini Notebook". It is the same tool, and the address still works.)
- **Claude Code**, the AI guide. Download it from https://claude.ai/download.

## Three steps to begin
1. **Download this kit.** On the GitHub page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**. Unzip the file into a folder you can find again, for example `Documents/research-kit`.
2. **Open the folder in Claude Code.** Start Claude Code, choose **Open folder** (or "open a project"), and select the unzipped folder. Full instructions are in `guide/01_install-and-open.md`.
3. **Type `/setup`** in the chat box and press Enter. The AI guide asks you a few questions and builds your workspace.

## Who does what

| You | The AI guide (Claude Code) | NotebookLM |
|---|---|---|
| Answer the setup questions | Builds and fills your workspace | Holds your documents |
| Put your documents in the folders | Records your sources | Answers each question from your documents only |
| Paste prompts into NotebookLM and copy the answers back | Prepares the exact prompts to paste | Shows quotes and pages for every point |
| Decide what matters | Saves answers word for word and logs every finding | |
